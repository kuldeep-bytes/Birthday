PARI BIRTHDAY WEBSITE — VERSION 2

Put 4 photos in images/ named pari1.jpg, pari2.jpg, pari3.jpg, pari4.jpg.
Put your MP3 in music/ named birthday.mp3.
Then open index.html in Chrome/Edge.

If your photos are PNG, replace .jpg with .png in index.html.
Music starts after clicking the opening button because browsers block autoplay.
